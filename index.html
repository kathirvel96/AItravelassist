Data & privacy

Saved places and itinerary entries are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. This demo does not require an account or collect personal information. Live weather requests send the searched place to Open-Meteo's public geocoding and forecast services.

About

TravelBuddy is a portfolio demo made with HTML, CSS, Bootstrap and vanilla JavaScript. The travel assistant is rule-based and does not pretend to be a live generative AI service.
